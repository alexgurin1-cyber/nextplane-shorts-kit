# NextPlane 15-second vertical pilots (TikTok, Instagram Reels), 2026-10-09

## Format spec (v0, pending Alex's review)

- 1080×1920, 30 fps, 12 to 15 s, -13.5 LUFS, AAC 48 kHz (Instagram rejects audio above 48 kHz).
- Three voice beats, about 30 to 36 words, Tyler Cash VO:
  1. **Hook (0 to 3 s):** the asking price and model on screen from the first frame (punch-in, no fade from black).
  2. **Turn (3 to 8 s):** one NextPlane report finding, animated (price history step chart, NTSB record, side-by-side history).
  3. **Action (about 2 s):** the report lookup card showing the N-number being typed into nextplane.us/aircraft-report.
  4. **End card (about 3 s):** the canonical card, with the verbatim CTA voiced over the lookup and the end card.
- One finding per video. No market statistics, no methodology.
- Platform safe zones: all text sits inside x 64 to 940 and y 210 to 1440, clear of the TikTok/Reels right-hand buttons and bottom caption area.
- Word-synced phrase captions at y ≈ 1400.
- Real photos of the subject model only, cropped to avoid other aircraft's registrations. An exact-length ElevenLabs Music bed starts on a downbeat at 0 s.
- Distribution: TikTok and Instagram only. YouTube and LinkedIn keep the ~90-second Short.
