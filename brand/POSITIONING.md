# NextPlane positioning — message house (Alex, 2026-11-16)

Read this before writing any script, caption, title, description, bio, email or page copy.
It replaces "The aircraft history report" positioning everywhere a buyer can see it.

## The line
**Know what to offer before you call the seller.**

## The closing line — unchanged, never reword
**Don't buy the story, buy the data.**
(End card, presentations, email sign-offs. The ad is "the story"; the report is "the data".)

## What NextPlane is, in one sentence
Negotiation intelligence on one specific airplane: enter the N-number of the airplane you found and,
for $29, see what to offer and what the ad won't tell you.

## The two halves of every report (use these names)
1. **What to offer** — what similar airplanes sell for and where this asking price sits; how long it has
   been for sale and every price cut; how the seller negotiates (a dealer's pattern, or an owner selling
   the only airplane they have).
2. **What the ad won't tell you** — its accident record, its ownership record (how many owners, how
   recently it changed hands) and how much it really flies, or whether it has been sitting.

Half 2 is the plain-language form of "ownership and usage intelligence". Never use that phrase in customer
copy; say "what the ad won't tell you" or name the facts themselves.

## Calls to action
- Site buttons: "Get the report — $29". Supporting line: "Check the N-number before you call."
- Video voiceover (verbatim, unchanged): "Before you call the broker, check the data at NextPlane.us."
- Seller-strategy clips keep their own approved CTA (see RUNBOOK_SHORTS15.md).
- Instagram / TikTok caption link line: "Check the N-number before you call — link in bio." (lib/np_link.py)
- Price wording: "$29 per airplane". Never put the price in logos, covers or brand assets.

## Retired — never in buyer-facing copy
"The aircraft history report", "history report", "Carfax for airplanes" / "Carfax-style", "Fly by the numbers",
"Aircraft Valuation & Market Intelligence", "intelligence report", "$9.99".
(History is a commodity — several sites give it away. Price history, time on market, seller behaviour and
live comparables are what only NextPlane has.)

## Words to prefer
offer · before you call · the seller · the ad · similar airplanes · price cut · time on market ·
how much it flies · accident record · owners

## Scope
Buyer-facing messaging: site, report emails, checkout, videos, captions, bios, ads, guides.
Lender, insurer and other corporate material keeps its own module framing (collateral and portfolio
reports) but must not call the product a history report.
