# How to Buy a Cessna 172 — POST COPY

**Title:** How to Buy a Cessna 172

**Description:**
The Cessna 172 is the most common airplane on the market, and that makes it the easiest one to overpay for. When dozens of Skyhawks are listed at similar prices, the real differences hide in the engine logbook, the airplane's past use and the list of recurring ADs.

In this episode we narrow 205 Skyhawks for sale down to a shortlist of ten, pick the one priced exactly at the market, and follow its NextPlane Aircraft Report question by question. You'll learn:
- how the 172 generations price against each other;
- why an airplane priced exactly at market can still cost 25% more per mile to fly;
- what a training history means for a used Skyhawk;
- how to build an offer from engine time and price-cut behavior instead of guessing.

Check any aircraft before you call the broker: https://nextplane.us
