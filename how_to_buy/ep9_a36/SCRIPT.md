### How A36s sell (on screen: panel-built cohort card, "A36s only")

How does that compare with other A36s?
We followed forty-two A36s first listed between late April and early August. These figures are for A36s only.

### Ownership and registration (on screen: Registration & Transfer Timeline, owner name blurred)

Long ownership is often a good sign. One owner usually means one maintenance story you can follow.
The seller is a dealer, not the owner.

### Mandatory safety fixes (on screen: Airworthiness & Maintenance Program card, source fine print blurred)
VO:
Mandatory safety fixes. The FAA calls these airworthiness directives, and an owner must comply with them.
Two are worth asking about here.
First, a Garmin directive requires a software update on the GFC five hundred autopilot. This airplane has one.
Second, a Bonanza directive covers cracks in the right aileron cable fittings on certain A36s.
Ask whether it applies to this serial number, and ask for the logbook entry either way.
