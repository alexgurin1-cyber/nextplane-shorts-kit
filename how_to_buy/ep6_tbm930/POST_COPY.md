# How to Buy a TBM 930 — Episode 6 — POST COPY

**Title:** How to Buy a TBM 930

**Description:**

A ten-year-old TBM 930 at the lowest ask on the market is not automatically a bargain. We walk one real airplane for sale through the NextPlane report to show what the price is really paying for.

You will learn how to place a TBM on the price ladder from the 700 to the 960, why the cheapest 2016 930 is the cheapest (hours, and an engine that is younger than the airframe), what a hot section inspection is and why its timing belongs in your offer, how to read a valuation and check the listings behind it, and how to turn price history, flight records and the seller's moves into an opening number.

Run any tail number before you call the broker: https://nextplane.us
