# How to Buy a Piper PA-28 — Episode 7 — POST COPY

**Title:** How to Buy a Piper PA-28

**Description:**

A 12,000-hour Piper Archer with a fresh engine and a new Garmin panel can ask $80,000 more than other high-hour Archers. We walk one real airplane for sale through the NextPlane report to show what that extra money is really buying, and what to check before you offer.

You will learn how to place a PA-28 on the price ladder from the Cherokee 140 to the late-model Archer, why airframe hours and engine hours pull the price in opposite directions, how to put a rough dollar figure on a fresh engine, why an airplane that barely flies needs a closer engine check, what the wing spar inspection directive means for high-hour PA-28s, and how to turn the seller's price history into an opening number.

Run any tail number before you call the broker: https://nextplane.us
