# How to Buy a Cessna 182 Skylane — POST COPY

**Title:** How to Buy a Cessna 182 Skylane

**Description:**
A cheap Skylane is usually cheap for a reason, and the reason is often the engine. The 1998 Cessna 182S in this episode asks $40,000 less than similar airplanes, but its engine has only about 300 hours left before its recommended overhaul. Once you count that, the "bargain" is priced about right.

We narrow 150 Skylanes for sale down to the 16 priced 182S models, pick one of the cheapest, and walk through its NextPlane Aircraft Report one question at a time. You'll learn:
- how Skylane prices step up by era, and why the 1997–2000 182S is its own price band;
- how to turn engine hours into dollars, so you can tell a real discount from a tired engine;
- what five quick price cuts, a company owner with a private seller, and an airplane that has barely flown each mean for you;
- how to build an opening offer and when to walk away.
