# How to Buy a Piper M350 — POST COPY

**Title:** How to Buy a Piper M350

**Description:**
A late-model Piper M350 can ask more than it cost new, because the price of a new one has climbed about 60% in three years. That makes the used market hard to read: is a four-year-old airplane at $1.475 million a fair deal, or just priced off the showroom?

In this episode we narrow 32 Mirages and M350s for sale down to a shortlist of four, pick the 2022 airplane in the middle, and follow its NextPlane Aircraft Report question by question. You'll learn:
- how the Mirage and M350 eras price against each other;
- how to test an asking price against real comparables instead of the newest listing;
- what a short ownership period, an engine younger than its airframe and recent flight activity each mean for a buyer;
- how to turn the estimate, the engine time and the seller's price history into an opening offer.
