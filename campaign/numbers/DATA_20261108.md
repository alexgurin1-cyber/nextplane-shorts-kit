## Why the template was tightened (row check)
- `original_asking_price_usd` is NOT always the first ask: on a raise it is often overwritten with the new price
  (N1157Z 139,000→175,000, N2500T 239,900→319,900, N8552R 250,000→299,000 all had p0 = current), and after a cut it can hold
  the cut price (N205G first 189,000 → 132,500 → 135,000 counted as "higher" though it is below its first ask; same N9352Q).
  Fix: first ask = old_price of the earliest `aircraft_price_history` row for the listing, else original_asking_price_usd.
- One-to-two-day first prices are scrape glitches (N717PG $200,000 for one day then $304,900; N5618K, N758KR 2 days).
  Fix: the first price must have held ≥ 3 days before the first change.
- Price sanity: asks under half the model median (≥3 for sale) are shares/partials/parse errors (N788LE 2022 SR22T at
  $114,148). Screened out of both numerator and base (2,418 → 2,374).
- Dropped by hand after reading the rows (first ask not credible): N31920 (Turbo Lance II, $158,000 typo while a
  concurrent ad for the same tail asked $268,500), N643XL ($45,000 first ask, +44%), N210BT ($95,000 for 5 days at the
  start of tracking, +58%), N15TA (bouncing 150k/250k/120k history).
